Rules

MAKING A COMMISSION

  2. Character model must be given by file, by link, or by point out a model I used before in one of my works.
  3. I do not accept controversial topics.
  4. All characters depicted in NSFW commissions must be confirmed as 18 years old or older.
  5. The client must provide written confirmation that they themselves are 18 years old or older before an NSFW commission begins.
  6. I reserve the right to refuse a commission if it makes me uncomfortable.
  7. The client is responsible for providing clear reference material (images, description, or mood board).
  8. If references are insufficient or unclear, timelines may be extended and I will request clarification before proceeding.
PAYMENTS
  1. Listed prices are estimates only — the final price is confirmed after the commission details have been presented and reviewed, and may be higher than given examples depending on complexity.
  2. Payment is split in two: 50% before work begins, the remaining 50% upon completion.
  3. Payment can only be made via Ko-Fi.
TIME
  1. I do not work with a fixed deadline, but commissions are typically completed within 1–2 weeks.
REVISIONS
  1. A reasonable number of revisions is included in the price, as long as the requested changes remain minor.
RIGHTS
  1. Every commission remains my property; I retain the right to post it publicly or as subscriber content.
  2. The client receives a copy of the finished image and may share or use it for non-commercial purposes only, unless a separate agreement states otherwise.
  3. If I am unable to complete the commission in its original form, I will propose an alternative and reduce the price accordingly.
  4. If I am unable to complete the commission at all, the full amount paid will be refunded.
  5. If the client cancels the commission after work has begun, the amount already paid is non-refundable.
  6. My work may not be used to train AI models.
  7. The client may not claim authorship of the commissioned work or resell it as their own.
  8. Commercial use (merchandise, advertising, resale, NFTs, etc.) is not included in standard commissions and requires a separate agreement.
